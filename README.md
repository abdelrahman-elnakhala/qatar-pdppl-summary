# Qatar PDPPL: One-Page Summary

A plain-language, article-referenced summary of Qatar's Personal Data Privacy Protection Law (Law No. 13 of 2016), written for a **fictional** Gulf media network, Horizon Gulf Media Network (HGMN). It supports the legal register in an ISO 27001 project and the breach-notification risk in the HGMN risk register.

> **Status: draft, not yet verified against the official text.** The summary was drafted from an unofficial English translation. The Arabic text in Official Gazette issue 15 of 2016 (almeezan.qa) controls. Work through `docs/verification_checklist.md` before relying on or citing this summary. This is not legal advice.

## What the summary covers

Scope and key terms, consent, individual rights, transparency, controller duties, security and breach notification, cross-border flows, special-nature data, children's websites, exemptions, direct marketing, penalties and complaints. A final section applies the law to HGMN's subscriber data, source data, suppliers and marketing.

## Method

1. Read the unofficial English translation article by article.
2. Rewrite each requirement in plain language with its article number.
3. Apply it to the fictional company's systems and data.
4. List open questions (regulator name, journalism exemption, later ministerial decisions) for verification.

## Limitations

- Based on a translation, not the Arabic original.
- Does not cover ministerial decisions or regulator guidelines issued under the law.
- Not legal advice.
